## ⚠️ Finding that may change your plan — Standard Chartered right-to-work

Search returned this, attributed to Standard Chartered's own early-careers FAQ:

> "You must have the permanent legal right to work in the country to which you are applying, meaning you do not need the Bank to apply for a work permit or visa, and that you are able to stay and work indefinitely in that country."

If that language is current and applies to the International Graduate Programme, **Standard Chartered does not sponsor** — and `application_plan.md` ranks it as your single strongest narrative fit with a submission target of Wed 26 Aug. That would be a large amount of effort spent against a closed door.

I could not open the FAQ page to confirm it, so this is `[B]`, not fact. **Confirm it at `https://www.sc.com/en/global-careers/early-careers/faq/` before you write anything for Standard Chartered.** It is the highest-value single check on this list.

# Suggested order of checks

Six page-opens, roughly one sitting. Do them in this order — the first one can delete a whole workstream.

1. `https://www.sc.com/en/global-careers/early-careers/faq/` — sponsorship. Can remove an employer from your plan entirely.
2. `https://www.revolut.com/careers` — confirm live 2027 postings, choose track from real titles.
3. `https://lifeattiktok.com/earlycareers/emea` — confirm live 2027 roles; check whether Madrid/Lisbon and language-specific roles exist.
4. `https://www.hsbc.com/careers/students-and-graduates/find-a-programme` — open or not; current stream names.
5. `https://careers.unilever.com/en/uk-and-ireland-early-careers` — is there a 2027 page yet.
6. `https://jobs.standardchartered.com/` — only if check 1 came back favourable.

## How to read this document

Every claim in this plan about **whether a scheme is open, when it closes, whether it sponsors visas, or which offices are hiring** is marked **VERIFY**. Nothing here asserts a live vacancy or a real deadline — those come from the employer's careers page and nowhere else.

The only dates I have committed to are **your own preparation deadlines**. Those are mine to suggest; the employers' are not.

The three groups below are ordered by *what you should do next*, not purely by fit score:

| Group | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Apply this week** | Highest fit. Verify the opening, then submit immediately. If a scheme turns out not to be open, the prep work still stands and you submit the day it opens. |
| **Apply after verification** | Genuine targets, but a specific unknown must be resolved before the effort is worth spending. |
| **Opportunistic** | Low marginal cost because they reuse work done for a higher-priority target. Submit only if the core five are already out the door. |

**Important caveat on "Apply this week":** the CSV says these schemes *typically* open in this part of the year. Typically is not the same as currently. Treat the group label as "these are first in the queue", not "these are confirmed open".
